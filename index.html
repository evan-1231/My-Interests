The Masters

The Masters is a yearly golf tournament held at Augusta National Golf Club in Augusta, Georgia. It's an extremely famous part of the game and history of golf. It's one of four men's majors, along with the U.S. Open, The Open, and the PGA Championship. Every year the winner is awarded a green jacket, along with prize money nearing 5.5 million dollars, and an honorary membership at the club.

Facts


  • The tournament is played annually in the first full week of April
  • The youngest masters winner was Tiger woods, in 1997 at just 21 years old
  • Jack Nicklaus holds the record for the most victories with six (1963, 1965, 1966, 1972, 1975, 1986)
